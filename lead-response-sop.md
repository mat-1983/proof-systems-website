# Proof Systems Lead Response SOP

## Purpose and service level

This is the manual response path for live website enquiries. It does not authorise autonomous sending.

- Review each new enquiry by the end of the next working day.
- Acknowledge a suitable enquiry within one working day of review. If a full answer needs longer, give one clear next-action date.
- Mat owns every V1 lead and must approve or send every external response.
- Keep exactly one named owner in restricted Notion, plus the same single next action and due date in Notion and the AGE-421 commercial row. Mat is the default V1 owner; the workbook has no owner column.
- Linear remains task tracking, not the lead database.

## Triage sequence

1. **Check safety first.** If the enquiry contains credentials, customer-identifiable data, special-category data, confidential documents or detailed sensitive commercial information, restrict access and do not copy the detail into Notion, AGE-421, Linear or local files. Ask for an anonymised restatement if needed.
2. **Match before creating.** Search the restricted Notion register using email, telephone and company. Reuse the existing `PS-L###` ID for the same relationship.
3. **Confirm contact scope.** Website `consent_contact` permits a response to this enquiry; it is not marketing, recording or AI-processing consent.
4. **Assess the route.** Check the recurring workflow, cost/friction, decision-maker access, safe evidence and suitable next step. Treat the website route as a recommendation, not an acceptance or booking.
5. **Complete the handoff.** Follow `crm-handoff.md`: identity/contact in restricted Notion; pseudonymous commercial state and next action in AGE-421.
6. **Prepare one response.** Use the matching template below, inserting a real date. Mat approves or sends it.

## Response templates

### Diagnostic request (`paid` / `diagnostic_requested`)

Subject: Your Proof Systems workflow enquiry

Thanks for outlining the workflow. I’ll review whether a focused diagnostic is the right next step and reply by [date]. This is a fit review, not confirmation of paid work. Please do not send confidential documents or identifiable customer information at this stage.

### Video or guide route (`video` / `video_routed`)

Subject: A practical first route for your workflow

Thanks for the context. This looks better suited to practical guidance before a paid step. The public guide library is still being produced, so I’ll either point you to a genuinely available resource or suggest another route by [date].

### Fit-call request (`call` / `call_requested`)

Subject: Your 15-minute fit-call request

Thanks for the request. I’ll review the workflow and confirm by [date] whether a 15-minute fit call is useful. The call is a short route check, not a free diagnostic or system-design session.

### Training enquiry (`training` / `training_enquiry`)

Subject: Your practical AI systems training enquiry

Thanks for the enquiry. The launch offer is a remote half-day workshop, preceded by a 15-minute fit call. I’ll review the team need and confirm whether that route fits by [date]; nothing is booked yet.

### Product or process route (`product` / `product_process_route`)

Subject: A simpler route may fit first

Thanks for the detail. An existing product, template or process change may be a better first step than custom work. I’ll review the gap and send a concise direction by [date].

### Short qualification

Before I suggest a next step, could you confirm: how often the workflow occurs; who owns it; the main failure or delay; the approximate time cost; and whether you can safely provide anonymised examples? Please do not send customer names, credentials or confidential documents.

### Sensitive-data correction

Thanks for your enquiry. It appears to include information that is more sensitive than we need at this stage. I will not copy that detail into the lead register. Please resend only an anonymised summary of the workflow, with customer names, credentials, confidential documents and sensitive personal or financial details removed.

### Not a fit

Thanks for explaining the workflow. I do not think Proof Systems is the right route for this at present, and I do not want to push you into a paid step that is unlikely to help. [Optional: one specific alternative.] I’ll close the enquiry unless you would like me to correct anything material.

## Follow-up, handoff and close

- After a response that needs a reply, set the next action to `First follow-up` at three working days.
- If there is no reply, send one first follow-up, then set `Final follow-up` for seven working days after the original response.
- If there is still no reply after the final follow-up, close as `no response` and set the retention-review date. Do not continue chasing.
- Record `not fit`, `no response`, `deferred` or `progressed` with a short, non-sensitive reason.
- A deferred lead must have an agreed review date. Without one, close it.
- A booked call records the calendar date and intended route; it is not duplicated as a Linear task.
- Before ending each Proof Systems working day, clear or deliberately reschedule every due/overdue open lead. Do not leave an open lead without an owner and dated action.

## Diagnostic recording boundary

Do not infer permission to record from a website enquiry or agreement to a diagnostic. Before any recording:

- explain the purpose, storage location, AI service(s), access and raw-recording retention period;
- obtain explicit recording consent and separate AI-processing consent;
- offer an equivalent non-recorded route;
- record the decisions in the approved diagnostic operating record; and
- keep recordings and transcripts out of Netlify, the Notion identity register, AGE-421 and this repository.

Withdrawal of contact permission stops optional contact. Withdrawal of recording or AI-processing consent is handled under the diagnostic procedure and must not be confused with CRM contact consent.
