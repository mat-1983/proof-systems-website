# General enquiry response SOP

Updated: 2026-08-29

## Response standard

Review new enquiries by the next working day. Match the business email and company in the restricted identity register before creating a record, then assign one owner, next action and due date.

## Suitable enquiry

Subject: Your Proof Systems workflow enquiry

> Thanks for outlining the workflow. It looks like a useful next conversation. I will suggest a short time to understand the work in more detail and whether a focused build, a paid diagnostic or training is the sensible first step. Please do not send confidential documents or identifiable customer information.

## More information needed

Subject: One question about your workflow enquiry

> Thanks for getting in touch. Before I suggest a next conversation, could you clarify [one specific, non-sensitive question]? Please keep the answer general and do not send confidential documents or personal customer information.

## Not suitable

Subject: Your Proof Systems workflow enquiry

> Thanks for taking the time to write. I do not think this is the right fit for Proof Systems at present. [Optional: one useful alternative.] I will close the enquiry unless you would like me to correct anything material.

## Consent boundary

The website consent permits review of and contact about this enquiry only. Obtain separate, explicit permission before recording a conversation, producing a transcript, using AI on customer material or reusing evidence publicly.
