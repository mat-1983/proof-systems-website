# Proof Systems Lead Response SOP

## Service level

- Review new website enquiries by the end of the next working day.
- Acknowledge suitable enquiries within one working day; if a full answer needs longer, give a clear next-action date.
- If no reply is received, follow up once after three working days and once after seven working days, then close the lead.
- Mat owns every V1 lead and next action. Linear remains task tracking, not the lead database.

## Triage

1. Check the enquiry contains no sensitive data. If it does, restrict access, do not copy it into other systems, and ask the sender to resend an anonymised version.
2. Confirm the route, evidence of a recurring workflow, cost or friction, decision-maker access and safe next step.
3. Record the minimum CRM fields defined in `crm-handoff.md`.
4. Send the matching acknowledgement below. Do not promise a diagnostic, workshop, call or build until Mat confirms fit.

## Templates

### Diagnostic request

Subject: Your Proof Systems workflow enquiry

Thanks for outlining the workflow. I’ll review whether a focused diagnostic is the right next step and reply by [date]. This is a fit review, not confirmation of paid work. Please do not send confidential documents or identifiable customer information at this stage.

### Video or guide route

Subject: A practical first route for your workflow

Thanks for the context. This looks better suited to practical guidance before a paid step. The public guide library is still being produced, so I’ll either point you to a genuinely available resource or suggest another route by [date].

### Fit-call request

Subject: Your 15-minute fit-call request

Thanks for the request. I’ll review the workflow and confirm by [date] whether a 15-minute fit call is useful. The call is a short route check, not a free diagnostic or system-design session.

### Training enquiry

Subject: Your practical AI systems training enquiry

Thanks for the enquiry. The launch offer is a remote half-day workshop, preceded by a 15-minute fit call. I’ll review the team need and confirm whether that route fits by [date]; nothing is booked yet.

### Product or process route

Subject: A simpler route may fit first

Thanks for the detail. An existing product, template or process change may be a better first step than custom work. I’ll review the gap and send a concise direction by [date].

### Short qualification

Before I suggest a next step, could you confirm: how often the workflow occurs; who owns it; the main failure or delay; the approximate time cost; and whether you can safely provide anonymised examples? Please do not send customer names, credentials or confidential documents.

### Not a fit

Thanks for explaining the workflow. I do not think Proof Systems is the right route for this at present, and I do not want to push you into a paid step that is unlikely to help. [Optional: one specific alternative.] I’ll close the enquiry unless you would like me to correct anything material.

## Handoff and close

- Every open lead must have one owner, one next action and one due date.
- Booked call: record the date and intended route; do not duplicate the appointment as a Linear task.
- Closed: record `not fit`, `no response`, `deferred` or `progressed`, with a short reason.
- Delete unprogressed enquiry data within 90 days.
