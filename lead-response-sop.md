# Founding Diagnostic Response SOP

Updated: 2026-07-12

## Response standard

Review new applications by the next working day. Match the business email and company in the restricted identity register before creating a record, then assign one owner, next action and due date.

## Suitable application

Subject: Your Proof Systems founding diagnostic application

> Thanks for outlining the workflow. It appears suitable for a closer fit conversation for the founding cohort. I will suggest a short time to confirm the scope and whether one of the four places is appropriate. This is not yet confirmation of a place or paid work. Please do not send confidential documents or identifiable customer information.

## More information needed

Subject: One question about your workflow application

> Thanks for applying. Before I assess the fit, could you clarify [one specific, non-sensitive question]? Please keep the answer general and do not send confidential documents or personal customer information.

## Not suitable

Subject: Your Proof Systems workflow application

> Thanks for taking the time to apply. I do not think this workflow is the right fit for the bounded founding diagnostic at present. [Optional: one useful alternative.] I will close the application unless you would like me to correct anything material.

## Consent boundary

The website consent permits assessment of and contact about this application only. Obtain separate, explicit permission before recording a conversation, producing a transcript, using AI on customer material or reusing evidence publicly.
