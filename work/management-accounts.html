Monthly reporting

Management Accounts

Produce a structured monthly reporting workbook with comparative views and controlled supporting schedules.

Synthetic demonstration

Finance and leadership teams · 53.9 seconds

The problem

A monthly pack is often assembled by copying figures between tabs.  Comparative views then drift, and the supporting schedules cannot be checked against the same period.

Who uses it

Finance and leadership teams need one structured workbook for the month, with comparative views and the schedules that support them.

The flow

  1. LedgerLink extracts controlled accounts data.
  2. Deterministic local processing combines that extract with verified live-system inputs.
  3. The profit and loss schedule compares current year, budget and forecast.
  4. Supporting overhead schedules and checks stay in the same workbook.

The demonstration

What this film shows

A monthly reporting workbook is opened on the profit and loss schedule, then on live project turnover and gross margin, then on supporting overhead schedules that stay in the same pack.

Design choices

The pack is a structured workbook, not a slide rebuild.  AI does not process the company accounting data.  Figures are accurate, repeatable and checkable against visible evidence.

Related systems

Accounts extracts come from LedgerLink.  Pipeline figures can be informed by Probables.
