Short and long-term views

Cashflow

Build short- and long-term cashflow views from connected source data.

Synthetic demonstration

Finance and leadership teams · 1 minute 18.7 seconds

The problem

Cash views are often rebuilt from applications, bank files and project schedules that do not share a source.  The short-term picture and the long-term forecast then tell different stories.

Who uses it

Finance and leadership teams need a near-term cash position and a longer view that can be traced back to connected source data.

The flow

  1. The finance console opens short-term and long-term cashflow from connected sources.
  2. The short-term workbook shows daily inflows, outflows and closing position.
  3. A project cost-value schedule is reviewed as source data.
  4. The long-term forecast sets out receipts, payments, facility position and checks.

The demonstration

What this film shows

The commercial finance console opens short-term and long-term cashflow.  A daily workbook, a project cost-value schedule and a longer forecast with facility checks are reviewed in turn.

Design choices

Short-term and long-term views stay in the same family of workbooks.  The point is connected source data, not a separately typed forecast.

Related systems

Application movement comes from Applications Ledger.  Accounts extracts come from LedgerLink.
