Selected systems

Working systems, shown as they run.

These are operator-built systems demonstrated with synthetic Northstar data.  Each card states who it is for, the operational outcome and the evidence in the film.  They are not a catalogue of products for sale and they are not external client case studies.

Frontline capture

Synthetic demonstration

SiteLog

Tradespeople and administrators

Outcome. Reliable weekly site records and payment-ready exports.

Evidence shown. A tradesperson records work against a selected project and location for the current week.  The entry appears in the weekly summary and a weekly invoice is generated.  An administrator then exports the selected week and reviews the project and user-management controls.

Project control

Synthetic demonstration

BudgetFlow

Site managers, quantity surveyors and accounts

Outcome. Weekly labour allocation, budget and variation control, overspend review and coded payment exports.

Evidence shown. A site manager allocates weekly labour costs to project budget locations and checks remaining value.  A quantity surveyor creates a controlled variation, builds a client quote and reviews an overspend request.  Accounts then generates a payment workbook with detailed records and a Sage-compatible nominal and cost-code export.
