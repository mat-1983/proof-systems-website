Selected systems

Working systems, shown as they run.

These are operator-built systems demonstrated with synthetic Northstar data.  Each example states who it is for, the operational outcome and the evidence in the film.  They are not a catalogue of products for sale and they are not external client case studies.

Frontline capture

SiteLog

Tradespeople and administrators

Outcome

Reliable weekly site records and payment-ready exports.

Evidence shown

A tradesperson records work against a selected project and location for the current week.  The entry appears in the weekly summary and a weekly invoice is generated.  An administrator then exports the selected week and reviews the project and user-management controls.

Explore SiteLog

Project control

BudgetFlow

Site managers, quantity surveyors and accounts

Outcome

Weekly labour allocation, budget and variation control, overspend review and coded payment exports.

Evidence shown

A site manager allocates weekly labour costs to project budget locations and checks remaining value.  A quantity surveyor creates a controlled variation, builds a client quote and reviews an overspend request.  Accounts then generates a payment workbook with detailed records and a Sage-compatible nominal and cost-code export.

Explore BudgetFlow

Commercial control

Applications Ledger

Commercial and finance teams

Outcome

One controlled route from application through certification, payment and retention.

Evidence shown

A commercial user opens live contracts, works in a ledger that keeps applications, certificates, payments and retention together, and uses role dashboards for due actions, variances and forecast hygiene.

Explore Applications Ledger

Project master

Central Project Register

Commercial leaders

Outcome

Portfolio overview with project-level drill-down.

Evidence shown

A portfolio of live, completed and pipeline projects is shown with identity and contract value.  Opening a live project reveals codes, contract terms and a hand-off into Applications Ledger.

Explore Central Project Register

Future work

Probables

Commercial and operational leaders

Outcome

A consistent pipeline of likely future work, including a new probable record.

Evidence shown

The pipeline lists likely future work with value, margin, status, owner and division.  A new probable is captured through a consistent form, including year-by-year value and retention, and joins the same list.

Outcome

Outcome

Short-term daily cash, project schedules and a longer forecast from connected sources.

Evidence shown

The commercial finance console opens short-term and long-term cashflow.  A daily workbook, a project cost-value schedule and a longer forecast with facility checks are reviewed in turn.
