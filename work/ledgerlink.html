Accounts extraction

LedgerLink

Extract controlled accounting data into reusable workbook outputs.

Synthetic demonstration

Finance teams · 1 minute 49.3 seconds

The problem

Period-end reporting often starts by exporting several files by hand and rebuilding them in a new workbook.  Checks then sit outside the extract, so it is hard to see what was kept, dropped or transformed.

Who uses it

Finance teams need a repeatable connection to accounts software, a chosen period and a trail of what each extract produced.

The flow

  1. The connection to accounts software is tested against the approved source.
  2. Company, period and datasets are selected.
  3. Extraction writes workbook outputs and records input, output and rejected counts.
  4. Evidence and drill-down link to each file.
  5. The resulting workbooks are opened for overheads, retention, project costs and related schedules.

The demonstration

What this film shows

The operator tests a connection to accounts software, queues the period datasets, runs the extraction and reviews evidence counts before opening the resulting workbooks.

Design choices

The interface shows the connection, the run and the evidence before anyone opens a spreadsheet.  Workbook outputs are a result of the extract, not the connection itself.

Related systems

LedgerLink supplies controlled accounts data.  Cashflow and Management Accounts use those outputs instead of rebuilding the source story by hand.
