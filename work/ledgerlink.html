Accounts extraction

The demonstration

What this film shows

The operator tests a connection to accounts software, queues the period datasets, runs the extraction and reviews evidence counts before opening the resulting workbooks.

The problem

Period-end reporting often starts by exporting several files by hand and rebuilding them in a new workbook.  Checks then sit outside the extract.

Who uses it

Finance teams need a repeatable connection to accounts software, a chosen period and a trail of what each extract produced.

The flow

  1. The connection to accounts software is tested against the approved source.
  2. Company, period and datasets are selected.
  3. Extraction writes workbook outputs and records input, output and rejected counts.
  4. Evidence and drill-down link to each file.
  5. The resulting workbooks are opened for overheads, retention, project costs and related schedules.

Design choices

Workbook outputs are a result of the extract, not the connection itself.  AI does not process the company accounting data.  Accuracy comes from deterministic calculations, controlled cut-offs, repeatable checks and visible evidence.

Related systems

LedgerLink supplies controlled accounts data.  Deterministic local processing then produces Cashflow and Management Accounts from that extract plus verified live-system inputs.
