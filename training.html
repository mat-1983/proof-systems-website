AI Team Training

Train your team to use AI on real workflows.

A remote or in-person half-day practical workshop for SME teams.  It is centred on the work you already do, not a generic tour of AI tools, and it is unpriced until we understand fit.

What it is

A remote or in-person half-day workshop for owners, operations, finance and admin leads, and department heads who own recurring work.

What it covers

Workflow selection, practical prompting, tool choice, responsible use, human checking and one team exercise on a real process.

What it is not

It is not a workflow diagnostic and it is not a software build.  Those remain separate starting routes when a specific system is needed.

After the session a team should be able to describe a recurring workflow, use AI tools more safely, and see where a human still has to check the result.
