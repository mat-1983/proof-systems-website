## 1. Intended outcome

Proof Systems becomes a permanent, credibility-led website for owner-led SMEs.
It should help an owner or senior operator recognise an important operational
problem, understand Mat's practical approach, inspect convincing evidence and
start a straightforward conversation.

The site is not a founding-cohort funnel, a generic AI agency, a construction
software catalogue or a content-led personal brand.

### Primary proposition

**Operational systems built around how a business really works.**

Proof Systems designs and builds practical software, controlled AI automation
and useful team training around real operational work.

### Primary action

**Discuss a workflow**

The action opens a short, privacy-conscious enquiry form. It does not require a
visitor to diagnose the solution, choose a service or book directly into a
calendar.

## 2. Visual direction

The experience should feel cinematic, technically capable and calm. It borrows
the chapter pacing, large typography and confident visual rhythm of the
SemiconBio reference without copying its identity or scientific aesthetic.

### Palette

- Near black: `#0A0B0D`
- Raised black: `#12151A`
- Warm cream: `#F3EEE4`
- Muted stone: `#AAA69E`
- Amber: `#F2A65A`
- Electric blue: `#43A7FF`
- Hairline: `rgba(243, 238, 228, 0.16)`

Amber represents people, judgement and the existing business. Electric blue
represents connected information, controlled automation and movement through a
system. Neither colour should become a neon wash.

### Type and composition

- Use a modern, highly legible sans-serif with large editorial headlines.
- Keep body copy comparatively narrow and conversational.
- Give each chapter one dominant idea and one dominant visual.
- Use chapter markers such as `01 / THE GAP`, not decorative pseudo-technical
  labels.
- Retain generous black space; do not fill every viewport with cards.
- Use solid warm-cream panels selectively to reset the visual rhythm.

### Motion language

- The opening visual begins as fragmented rows, spreadsheet cells and isolated
  status labels. As the visitor scrolls, lines connect them into one controlled
  workflow.
- Sticky chapter transitions may pin one visual while short statements change.
- SVG paths, status pulses and restrained parallax support the explanation.
- Homepage proof films use poster-first, lightweight muted teaser loops only
  when visible and motion is allowed.
- Full demonstrations remain user-initiated and controlled on story pages.
- Do not hijack scrolling, hide the pointer, add an intro loader or require
  WebGL.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` removes scroll-linked movement and presents
  the same content in its final readable state.
- Mobile uses simple fades and static diagrams rather than reproducing every
  desktop transition.

> Begin with a focused build or automation around one bounded outcome.

> Begin with a paid workflow diagnostic: understand the present process,

> Improve the next connected part, or continue with ongoing systems and
> automation support. Growth is earned by the result, not assumed at the start.

### Story-page pattern

1. System name and one-sentence operational outcome.
2. Clear `Synthetic demonstration` label.
3. Problem: what was fragile or manual.
4. Users: who needs the workflow.
5. Flow: three to five visible stages.
6. Film with poster, native controls, captions and accessible summary.
7. Design choices: why the system is deliberately simple.
8. Relationship to other systems where relevant.
9. No invented performance claim or client result.
10. `Discuss a workflow` action.

## 8. Review checks

Approve the design only if:

- the hero describes operational systems rather than an AI agency;
- AI automation and training are visible but supporting;
- a non-technical SME owner can explain the offer and next step within one
  minute;
- SiteLog, BudgetFlow and finance demonstrate distinct operational breadth;
- all eight systems have a clear story route;
- the motion earns its place and disappears cleanly under reduced motion;
- the form feels like a conversation rather than an application;
- synthetic proof cannot be mistaken for an external client case study;
- the Pantera chapter cannot render without approved content;
- no Xonetic or prospective-client claim appears;
- there is no publication, hosting or contact action in the implementation
  authority.
