Funnel logic

Not every problem should become a paid diagnostic.

The videos are the first filter. They should help the wrong-fit visitor get useful help for free, and help the right-fit visitor arrive with a clearer workflow, better examples, and stronger intent.

Self-serve

Some visitors only need language and examples.

If the problem is early, low-risk or still vague, the useful next step is a video series. That builds trust without forcing a paid step too soon.

Good fit when they need to understand workflows, APIs, AI agents, automation trade-offs or what to test first.

High intent

Some visitors have a workflow worth diagnosing.

If the pain is expensive, repeated, document-heavy or dependent on one person, the qualifier should move them towards a diagnostic or a call.

Good fit when there is a live workflow, a clear cost of delay and enough access to map the process properly.
