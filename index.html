Operator-built systems

Sixteen years improving how a real business runs.

I did not start with AI.  I started by running operations and building better ways to manage the work.  Across 16 years inside an operating construction business, including time as its managing director, I have designed systems around the recurring problems faced by finance, commercial and operational teams.

Since February 2025 this has been my full-time focus.  Software and AI now let me connect more of those workflows, automate controlled steps and make the right evidence visible to the people responsible for decisions.

Finance and reporting

Operational proof

Two connected operator-built systems.

These are synthetic demonstrations of operator-built systems.  Construction is the evidence context, not a limit on the workflows this diagnostic can review.  SiteLog supplies the recurring weekly records, and BudgetFlow controls their downstream allocation and processing.  They are not external customer case studies.

Synthetic demonstration

SiteLog

Tradespeople enter current-week site records.  Administrators keep the project and user controls and export the week.

What this video shows

A tradesperson records work against a selected project and location for the current week.  The entry appears in the weekly summary and a weekly invoice is generated.  An administrator then exports the selected week and reviews the project and user-management controls.

Synthetic demonstration

BudgetFlow

Managers allocate weekly costs against project budgets.  Quantity surveyors control variations, quotes and overspends.  Accounts produce the payment exports.

What this video shows

A site manager allocates weekly labour costs to project budget locations and checks remaining value.  A quantity surveyor creates a controlled variation, builds a client quote and reviews an overspend request.  Accounts then generates a payment workbook with detailed records and a Sage-compatible nominal and cost-code export.
