Operator first

I started with the operation, not the technology.

I’m Mat Glendenning. My experience combines operational leadership, systems design and hands-on delivery. Across sixteen years inside an operating construction business, including time as its managing director, I built and improved systems used by finance, commercial and operational teams.

In 2016, I also co-founded Jobhawk, a mobile-first construction recruitment marketplace. We raised £1m+, with the Isle of Man Government as lead investor. That experience sharpened my understanding of product fit, adoption and the operating realities that determine whether technology succeeds.

Proof Systems applies that operator’s view to owner-led businesses. I begin with the work, the people responsible for it and the evidence they need.
