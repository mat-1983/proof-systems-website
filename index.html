01B / FIT

You no longer have to choose between a spreadsheet and software built for somebody else's business.

Off-the-shelf products are designed for a broad market.  Smaller firms often pay recurring per-user, module or bolt-on costs, live with features they do not need, still miss the few functions that matter, and end up reshaping useful working practices around the software.

AI-assisted development has materially reduced the cost and time of a small, well-bounded bespoke operational system.  Architecture, testing, security, judgement and ownership remain essential.  AI helps the build; it does not replace them, and it does not process company accounting data in the finance workflow shown here.

Broad software

A larger product, recurring add-ons and a process shaped around the vendor.

The missing operational layer

The few controls, hand-offs and evidence the business actually depends on.

A focused bespoke system

Buy the commodity foundation.  Build only the operational layer that is specific to the business.

I would advise against a build where a commodity platform already fits, the problem is not yet understood, or the cost of change would outweigh the operational gain.
