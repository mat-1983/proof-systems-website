Software should follow the work

When software doesn’t fit, the work finds a way around it.

Sometimes the process is reshaped to suit the software. Sometimes the awkward parts move into spreadsheets, email and separate files.

The team keeps the work moving. The information gets left behind: copied entry, detached approvals, competing versions and reports that arrive behind the work.

The problem is the break in connected information and accountability between the tools.

Follow one piece of work

One request, moving through four hand-offs.

  1. 01
    The request arrives

    Its source and requirement are clear while they are together.

  2. 02
    The records diverge

    Delivery moves on while approval and accounts follow their own copies.

  3. 03
    The approval detaches

    Ownership, status and history now have to be reconstructed.

  4. 04
    The record stays connected

    Source, requirement, owner, status, approval and history travel with the work.

The missing layer

Keep the software that works. Connect the work that falls between it.

I trace how the work really moves: where information begins, who owns the next action, what must be checked and what evidence needs to survive.

Then I build the smallest shared layer that keeps its information, owner and history attached.
