Operator-built systems

Sixteen years improving how a real business runs.

I did not start with AI.  I started by running operations and building better ways to manage the work.  Across 16 years inside an operating construction business, including time as its managing director, I have designed systems around the recurring problems faced by finance, commercial and operational teams.

Since February 2025 this has been my full-time focus.  Software and AI now let me connect more of those workflows, automate controlled steps and make the right evidence visible to the people responsible for decisions.
