Privacy notice

This notice covers the Proof Systems website qualifier and direct enquiries. It was last updated on 12 July 2026.

Who is responsible

Proof Systems is operated by Mat Glendenning in the United Kingdom. Mat is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. Contact mat@proofsystems.co.uk for privacy questions or requests.

What is collected and why

The qualifier collects your name, business contact details, company and role, plus the workflow information you choose to provide. It is used only to assess your enquiry, suggest a suitable next step and respond to you. Please do not submit customer names, passwords, confidential documents, detailed financial data, special-category information or other sensitive personal data.

How it is handled

Website enquiries are processed through Netlify Forms and may be delivered to Proof Systems by email. Access is limited to Mat and the service providers needed to receive and store the enquiry. Information is not sold and is not used for unrelated marketing.

Retention

Enquiries that do not progress are deleted within 90 days. If an enquiry becomes active work, relevant contact and project records may be retained for the duration of the relationship and for any period required for legal, tax or contractual records. Browser session storage used by the route-review page is temporary and stays on your device until the tab or browser session ends.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent to further contact, by emailing mat@proofsystems.co.uk. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office if you believe your information has been mishandled.
